What is a group flight booking?

A group booking reserves seats for 10 or more passengers travelling together on the same flights. Airlines price and manage these bookings separately from individual tickets. Group bookings usually include a negotiated fare, a deposit to hold the seats, and a later deadline for passenger names and final payment.

Tips for a smooth group booking

  • Book early. Airlines limit how many group seats they sell on each flight. Aim for 3 to 11 months ahead.
  • Be flexible where you can. Moving your dates by a day, or choosing a nearby airport, can lower the group fare a lot.
  • Know your headcount range. Give us a minimum and maximum. Many contracts allow some attrition before a deadline.
  • Collect names early. Names must match government ID exactly, so start collecting them early.
