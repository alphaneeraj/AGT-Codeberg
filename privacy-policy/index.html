Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 2026-09-28

This Privacy Policy explains how Airfare Services USA LLC ("Airlines Group Travel", "we", "us") collects and uses information on airlinesgrouptravel.codeberg.page.

Information we collect

  • Quote requests: your name, email, phone number, travel details and any message you send through our forms.
  • Communications: records of calls and emails with our specialists.
  • Technical data: the page you submitted a form from. This site uses no advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send group travel quotes and to contact you about your request.
  • To make bookings you ask for, which means sharing passenger details with airlines and travel suppliers.
  • To meet legal, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with airlines and suppliers needed for your booking, with service providers who store data for us (for example, Google Workspace), or when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep quote requests for as long as we need them to serve you and meet legal obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or opt out of follow-up contact, by emailing info@airlinesgrouptravel.com. California residents may have extra rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

Hosting

This website is hosted on Codeberg Pages. Codeberg may process server logs such as IP addresses to operate the service.
