How to Book Flights for Group with Allegiant Air?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 12, 2026 · 3 min read

Trips are scheduled for distinct purposes and can be arranged smoothly with Allegiant Air. But when multiple companions seek to book flight tickets, they have to choose a travel option that suits their needs. Therefore, its group travel service is an ideal option for this kind of travel. It allows members setting out to reach the same destination to fly together. With the Allegiant Air group travel option, passengers may also avoid spending extra time arranging each ticket separately and can avail special fares for their shared journey. So, this lets groups fly with more ease and without breaking the bank. But arranging these journeys becomes easy when travelers consider the information shown in the following headings.

Does Allegiant Air Allow Group Booking?

Indeed! Allegiant Air allows group booking. Due to this offering, a number of travelers will be able to travel in a single PNR. Furthermore, they will be able to curate their itinerary at reduced rates. Not only that, when they do an Allegiant Air group booking, bespoke services and amenities are offered to them. It includes flexibility in name changes, payment, and convenient seating options. So, travelers seeking to explore the common place with their companions can opt for the group booking feature of the airline.

How to Book Flights for Groups with Allegiant Air?

Travelers looking to reduce the effort of arranging flight tickets can explore the group booking facility offered by Allegiant Air. The airline allows them to begin the process through different channels. They can either use the office email address to book an Allegiant Air group flight, which is group.bookings@allegiantair.com, or its online form. One can find it on the official website of the airline and share the details of their planned journey at once. The following steps explain how to proceed with the online inquiry.

  1. Head to Allegiant Air's official website to start arranging the group journey.
  2. Look for "Groups / Charters" and open that option.
  3. Further, tap "REQUEST A QUOTE" and choose the suitable option that matches the group size, which is under 40 or 40 and above.
  4. Then, add the requested travel and contact details to the inquiry form and send it.
  5. After that, within a few working days, a quotation will be shared by email.
  6. If the offer works, confirm its terms and make the payment as instructed.
  7. Once the full amount has been paid, send the passenger details.
  8. In the end, the party leader will receive the flight confirmation by email.

How to Get Group Discounts on Allegiant Air?

Traveling with known faces on Allegiant Air may aid passengers in managing their flight expenses more effectively. This is due to their group rates, which are offered when more than 9 adults fly on the same flight segment. Besides, Allegiant Air group rates are available for any party traveling domestically or internationally. But certain conditions apply to avail such offers, and one can learn them from the points illustrated below.

  • Secure shared flights as soon as the airline allows for reasonable fares.
  • Try to reserve a ticket that takes off early in the morning.
  • When a group travels late at night, they can also get discounted fares.
  • On working days, the airline may lower the ticket prices.
  • Not flying during holidays or festivals can also be a better choice.

Conclusion

Thus, organizing trips for a large number of flyers is flawless on Allegiant Air. But travelers have to opt for the airline's group service to begin such journeys. This Allegiant group travel service also provides flyers with numerous perks, along with discounted fares. But to acquire them, following the details shared above is quite essential. A traveler considering this information could also avoid any complications while reserving group flights.
