How Can I Book a Group Flight Ticket on Flynas?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 24, 2026 · 3 min read

Travelers can plan an excursion across the world with a leading low-cost carrier in Saudi Arabia, Flynas. This airline not only lets passengers fly across its wide network of routes but also offers them a plethora of services. Among them, group travel is one that is useful for travelers about to fly for common events with a number of members. Besides, the Flynas group travel service can be chosen to prepare both international and domestic expeditions. But understanding the requirements and conditions of using this feature is crucial for having a more compelling experience. From the headings illustrated below, one can get insights into these.

What Are the Group Booking Terms and Conditions on Flynas?

On Flynas, taking a tour with a group is considered exciting and comfortable. But a party with multiple passengers will be able to fly on this airline by adhering to some essential rules. One can learn them from the Flynas group booking terms and conditions page. Before accepting the offer, travelers can go through these conditions and proceed to confirm their booking more smoothly. Additionally, one can find those policies in the points shown below.

  • Group reservations on Flynas are accepted for 10 or more passengers traveling to the same destination on the same flight segment.
  • The airline may arrange seats together at no extra cost, depending on availability.
  • Flyers can get the combined baggage allowance as per airline rules.
  • Payment options and due dates vary according to the scheduled flights and booking terms.
  • Traveler names might be revised after the initial reservation, subject to applicable conditions.
  • When tickets are cancelled, it may result in penalties. After payment or after receiving tickets, refunds are subject to availability.

How Can I Book a Group Flight Ticket on Flynas?

Travelers seeking to go on a leisure or business trip with their companions can choose Flynas. This airline provides a very uncomplicated method to book such excursions on its different routes. One can submit their group booking request to this airline via email. Furthermore, the official email address to secure a Flynas group ticket is Groupsdesk@flynas.com. One has to mention the destination, travel date, party size, contact number, party leaders' names, and other required details in this email. After sending the request, a traveler can follow the steps listed below.

  1. Once the airline receives the request, it will review it.
  2. After that, a price quotation will be forwarded by email.
  3. The group leader has to confirm it within the time prescribed in the offer.
  4. Thereafter, proceed to complete the transaction using the available payment method.
  5. Further, one may have to share the names of all members.
  6. Once done, the airline will send the final tickets.

Does Flynas Offer Group Discounts?

Absolutely! Group discounts by Flynas are offered across its network. But in order to benefit from these special fares, travelers have to meet the special guidelines and conditions. The most important factor to get a Flynas group discount is the headcount requirement, which is 10 or more pax. Along with this, all members who are part of the same group have to fly on the same route for the common purpose. But these discounted fares may be offered based on availability and other factors.

Conclusion

Thus, Flynas facilitates group trips with utmost comfort and ease. Not only this, but when a number of flyers travel together under one itinerary on its flights, they get to receive discounted fares and unbeatable facilities. But Flynas group booking flights have to be done by complying with some important rules, which are illustrated above. Along with those conditions, one can learn the procedure to obtain group flight tickets and discounts.
