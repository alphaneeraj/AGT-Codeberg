How to Book Cabo Verde Airlines Tickets for a Group?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 1, 2026 · 3 min read

An expedition scheduled with Cabo Verde Airlines is bound to be compelling and comfortable. This airline is recognized by travelers for its exclusive travel services that cater to different passenger trip requirements, including groups. When travelers have to take a Cabo Verde Airlines flight with their companions, they can choose its group travel service. With this, they will be able to secure a larger block of seats on the same flight to the same destination. Moreover, there are a number of advantages that make Cabo Verde Airlines group travel option more relevant and convenient for their collective journey. Learning more about this travel feature of the airline could be worthwhile for planning a shared journey. Therefore, travelers can refer to the headings highlighted below to grab that information.

What Are the Advantages of Group Booking on Cabo Verde?

Travelers flying with Cabo Verde Airlines reach their destination in full comfort while benefiting from many travel features. Not only can individual flyers avail such an experience, but also those flying in a group. Even travelers using the Cabo Verde Airlines group booking option enjoy extra facilities and benefits throughout their journey. By reading the following points, one will be able to learn about the advantages of group booking.

  • Enjoy special group fares that can make the trip more economical.
  • Get flexible options for submitting or changing passenger names.
  • Benefit from convenient payment options for the booking.
  • Group bookings can offer better flexibility and easier travel management.
  • Save time by handling the party reservation in one booking.

How to Book Cabo Verde Airlines Tickets for a Group?

Seats for groups of individuals can be reserved under the same flight segment with Cabo Verde Airlines effortlessly. For that, travelers have to submit the form with the mandatory details. Moreover, they will be able to find the Cabo Verde Airlines Group Travel Form by accessing its official website. There are very simple steps to reach the airline's website and complete the reservation. Travelers can learn them in detail by taking a look at the steps below.

  1. Visit the official Cabo Verde Airlines website.
  2. Go to the homepage and opt for "Group Booking."
  3. Then, enter all the required details in the booking form and click "Submit."
  4. Now, the airline will send a quotation.
  5. After receiving the offer, review and confirm it before the deadline.
  6. Further, pay the ticket amount using one of the available payment methods.
  7. Thereafter, upload the names and required details of all passengers.
  8. Finally, get the confirmed flight tickets at the registered email address.

Does Cabo Verde Airlines Offer Group Discounts?

Indeed! Group discounts are offered by Cabo Verde Airlines. With such special offers from the airline, multiple companions will not have to worry about their budget. However, the airline may make these discounts available for groups that have more than 9 passengers with a common travel plan. Aside from this, arranging flights in advance can increase the chances of availing Cabo Verde Airlines group discounts without adding extra services. Also, flyers might need to choose seasons to fly when travel demand is low.

Conclusion

So, group travel is a unique offering of Cabo Verde Airlines. Passengers using it can fly with their known ones seamlessly and acquire an array of travel benefits. But shared journeys have to be arranged by complying with the airline's stipulations and guidelines. When these terms are followed, no one faces trouble while making their reservation and flying together with their companions. On the other hand, flyers can contact the airline's live agent for any issues with the group booking or related matters about its services.
