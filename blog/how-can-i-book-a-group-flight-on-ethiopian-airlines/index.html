How Can I Book a Group Flight on Ethiopian Airlines?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · September 29, 2026 · 3 min read

Passengers can embark on an enhanced escape with Ethiopian Airlines. This airline is known to offer a wide range of services on its distinct routes. Moreover, travelers can plan a solo or collective trip with these travel features. In such cases, flyers offer special group travel services for those passengers who prefer traveling together. But to get Ethiopian Airlines group travel tickets, there are requirements for travelers seeking to complete their journey together. Before planning the trip, one must consider those factors. By doing so, accessing the airline's exclusive travel facilities and special fares makes air travel easier. Besides, the information that is required to arrange shared voyages is as follows.

What Is the Group Booking Policy on Ethiopian Airlines?

Travelers seeking to fly together with their companions can take an Ethiopian Airlines flight. Since such travel includes several passengers, the airline follows certain conditions to keep the reservation properly structured. The Ethiopian Airlines group booking policy makes the reservation process smoother by letting passengers know every requirement of flying together. Also, taking a quick look at them beforehand can avoid confusion during the booking or later on. The important points related to these policies are mentioned below.

  • The airline allows more than 9 companions to fly in a group to the same destination, and they have to fly through one or more flight segments under the same reservation.
  • A single reservation can include up to 99 flyers. But seats can be pre-reserved for only 50 passengers at a time. If the itinerary has all 99 members, two PNRs have to be created for issuing the seats.
  • Names need to be submitted 60 days before departure. If a traveler cannot provide the name by that date, the name may be submitted 30 days before takeoff.
  • A group itinerary may also be cancelled by the traveler when the option is mentioned in the airline's offer.
  • The reservation may no longer qualify as group travel when the cancellation reduces the party size to 9 flyers or fewer.

How Can I Book a Group Flight on Ethiopian Airlines?

Ethiopian Airlines provides travelers with a dedicated way to put forward an inquiry for group travel on the basis of their proposed itinerary. This airline lets eligible passengers visit its official website and submit the required information to initiate the request. The Ethiopian Airlines group flight booking request can then be reviewed by the concerned team, after which a quotation may be shared according to the travel requirements. The complete method for submitting the group travel request is outlined in the steps below.

  1. Head to the group portal of Ethiopian Airlines.
  2. New users can create an account, while registered users can sign in directly.
  3. Afterward, find the group travel option and access the form.
  4. Enter the required information in the form and send the request.
  5. The airline will check the request and return a quotation for the trip.
  6. Go through the offer and accept it before the stated expiry time.
  7. Then, pay the initial deposit first, followed by the remaining amount as instructed.
  8. Once the payment is done, add the names of all passengers within the given deadline.
  9. After the final details are received, the group itinerary or tickets will arrive by email.

Does Ethiopian Airlines Offer Group Discounts?

Indeed! Travelers can acquire group discounts on Ethiopian Airlines. But for that, they have to fly with multiple individuals. Basically, these discounts apply to the Ethiopian Airlines group booking, which is made for 10 or more familiar flyers. Not only special fares, but the airline also offers groups flexible name changes and submission options. Also, flyers might be able to secure their reservation with a small deposit.

Conclusion

Thus, planning a shared journey with Ethiopian Airlines becomes easier once the essential group travel aspects are understood. The applicable requirements, booking provisions and related formalities give travelers a better understanding of how their journey can be taken forward. By going through the Ethiopian Airlines group ticket booking details discussed above, one can prepare their plans with greater clarity and keep the necessary arrangements in order. This also makes it easier to handle the reservation without unnecessary confusion along the way.
