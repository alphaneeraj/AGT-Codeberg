How Can I Book a Group Flight with Swiss Air?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 19, 2026 · 3 min read

Compelling journeys are crafted and initiated with SWISS International Airlines. It is the national airline of Switzerland that offers diverse services to make air trips convenient for its passengers. One can even accommodate multiple individuals under one itinerary by opting for its group option. Further, the Swiss Air group booking is a premium facility that is offered with combinations of benefits and perks to each involved individual. Therefore, it is an ideal option to make a trip organized and free from regular complications. To know its attributes more deeply, have a look below.

Does Swiss Air Offer Group Discounts?

Definitely! Group discounts for travelers are offered by Swiss Air. Through this, over 9 pax can fly together on the same flight by paying less than individual fares. But the savings offered through Swiss Airlines Group discounts may differ according to the chosen route, booking timing, travel season, and availability. So, travelers must consider these elements along with other necessary guidelines of the airline.

What Are Swiss Air Group Travel Terms & Conditions?

A tour planned on a Swiss Air flight with companions gives an awe-inspiring experience. But the airline enforces certain guidelines to create group trips. Travelers are required to follow these Swiss Airlines group booking policies before beginning to request a flight. By doing so, they will be able to send their booking request while keeping in mind every essential requirement. Furthermore, those group travel terms and conditions are disclosed below.

  • A minimum of 10 passengers must travel together on both the onward and return flights.
  • Children under 12 need an adult to accompany them for traveling on Swiss flights.
  • Travelers have to complete the full booking payment within 7 days of the reservation.
  • The airline requires every passenger’s name at least 14 days before departure. Missing the deadline can lead to cancellation fees.
  • Travelers might not be able to make changes to the dates of their travel and itinerary.
  • Passenger names can be changed free of charge before ticket issuance. After tickets are issued, each name change costs $113.

How Can I Book a Group Flight with Swiss Air?

A flight on Swiss Air can be booked by groups as well, rather than only individual travelers. Moreover, the airline offers the simplest way to make a group reservation through its official website. But eligible travelers may only be able to request Swiss Air group bookings through this channel. Also, they can view the guidelines and benefit from discounted fares by sending their reservations via the website. Below, the steps for booking a group flight are shared.

  1. Start by heading to the official SWISS website.
  2. From the main menu, locate the “Book & Prepare” category.
  3. Under this section, look for “Group travel & Charter.”
  4. Tap “Book now” and then “To the group travel request form."
  5. Then, provide the necessary travel information through the form and send the request.
  6. Soon, the airline checks the request and shares a quotation.
  7. Approve the proposed fare before it expires and settle the initial deposit.
  8. Afterward, clear the remaining amount before the due date.
  9. Then, give the names and necessary particulars of every member flying in a party.
  10. In the end, the airline will email the tickets and flight itinerary.

Conclusion

Thus, organizing a flight for several known flyers is convenient with group features. Swiss Air provides it for eligible groups to let them fly together with ease. Aside from this, the information related to this travel option and its fares has been explained above. Exploring these details in advance gives travelers a clearer idea of the available booking terms and possible savings, making it easier to prepare for their trip together.
