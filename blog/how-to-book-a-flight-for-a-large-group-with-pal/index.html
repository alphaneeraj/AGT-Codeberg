How to Book a Flight for a Large Group with PAL?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 6, 2026 · 3 min read

Organizing a journey with larger groups feels like a different kind of excitement in one's heart. This can be initiated with the oldest commercial airline in Asia, Philippine Airlines. This airline is good at offering exclusive services to its passengers at a reasonable rate. Moreover, traveling with a large number of passengers can be done through its group flights. These flights are provided under the Philippine Airlines group travel program that allows one to travel together with their companions. Along with this, they also provide several premium perks that are usually not offered on regular bookings. Other than that, one can generate their tickets through a straightforward method. Thus, to learn about all this, one can read the outlines below.

Does Philippine Airlines Do Group Booking?

Yes, PAL does offer a group booking program to its passengers. Traveling groups that are looking for a comfortable service from the airline can choose this one. With its shared flights, one can fly together with their companions using a single itinerary. Moreover, with these Philippine Airlines group bookings, passengers get to enjoy several deals and amenities that are offered for the journey. Therefore, flying on this airline is definitely going to provide several memorable experiences.

What Are the Group Booking Policies on Philippine Airlines?

Traveling with Philippine Airlines is much smoother when one is aware of the conditions of the airline. By doing so, travelers can get access to several deals and offerings of the airline that are only provided on group travel. Moreover, these stipulations are easy to align with and are applied to each party member. Thus, to gain more information about this, one can read the following guidelines.

  • A flyer needs at least 10 passengers flying together to book as a group.
  • Passengers' details must be submitted before the due date.
  • Flight seats will be secured only when the full payment is made.
  • Cancellation must be made 24 hours before departure to avoid unnecessary fees.

How to Book a Flight for a Large Group with PAL?

Reserving the group flight on PAL for numerous flyers is simple because of the online web portal. Through this method, the party leader can make the reservation for all the members of the party. Moreover, with this Philippine Airlines Group Booking page, they can also get to know about the current deals and offerings of the airline. Other than that, the flyer only has to fill out a form to get the tickets with no hassle. Thus, one can learn the booking steps from the pointers below.

  1. Reach out to the official website of Philippine Airlines.
  2. Go to the "Book" icon and then to the "Group booking" option.
  3. Now, click on the first link displayed on the screen.
  4. After this, choose the "Group Bookings" option.
  5. Pick the required category, either "New Group Booking Request" or "Existing Booking Request."
  6. Now, complete the form by filling out all the details and submit it.
  7. After a while, the group quote will be shared via email, along with the airfare.
  8. Accept it before the given deadline and proceed to make the payment.
  9. Thereafter, share the names of all the party members.
  10. At the end, get the tickets sent to the registered email.

Conclusion

Hence, these group flights with PAL are not as complicated as assumed by travelers. Moreover, this airline provides discounts to the group that follows its terms and conditions. Further, the travelers get to relax knowing that the airline offers an easily accessible method to book flights through the online platform. Besides, for any doubts or queries, one can reach out to the group desk team.
