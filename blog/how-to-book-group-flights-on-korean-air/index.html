How to Book Group Flights on Korean Air?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 22, 2026 · 3 min read

Preparing a single itinerary for a trip with multiple flyers has become convenient with Korean Air. Here, passengers are facilitated with the group facility, which lets them design a trip with a large number of individuals under a single itinerary. Aside from this, reserving seats is also effortless with the Korean Air group travel feature. Not only that, but flyers can also access many other facilities, such as discounted rates. However, this access to the service can only be granted to passengers when they meet the minimum guidelines of the carrier. In addition to that, one can comply with these rules by referring to the below-mentioned titles, which also include some other details about the topic as well.

What Are the Korean Air Group Booking Rules?

On Korean Air, flying towards a common destination with multiple companions has become smooth for flyers. The airline provides the group travel feature to do that, which can be accessed after adhering to the minimum criteria of the carrier. Furthermore, by following the group flying policy, one can easily request Korean Air group reservations without going through any complicated procedure. Along with that, the voyages can become affordable for travelers, as fulfilling the requirements can also unlock reduced fares. So knowing about the guidelines would unlock a number of exciting offers for the flyers, which are outlined in the given pointers.

  • A trip with companions can be planned when there are more than nine passengers in the group.
  • Passengers must also remain together while being on the same route and destination.
  • Modification requests to bookings or travel details can be made through the agencies themselves since the reservations are handled by them.
  • One should also carry their original and valid ID proofs to the airport on the day of travel to pass the security checks smoothly.

How to Do Group Booking on Korean Air?

Shared expeditions with large groups can be booked comfortably on Korean Air. The carrier allows individuals to request bookings through an authorized travel agency. In this way, a Korean Air group booking would be registered under a unified system. This allows individuals to bypass the lengthy procedures of arranging separate tickets for every member of the party. Further more, obtaining tickets through travel agencies requires individuals to follow certain steps, which are listed in the given section.

  1. Initiate the bookings by finding a reliable travel agency.
  2. Transmit the trip details to them and include all the other travel essentials and requirements as well.
  3. Passengers will then be provided with an official quote, which has a certain time period within which it should be accepted by the travelers.
  4. After accepting the offer, one should complete the payment process and then submit the names of every party member.
  5. Lastly, one can collect the confirmed travel documents from the registered email account of the flyer.
