A journey spent with close members provides numerous memorable moments. However, curating such trips seems to be tough and requires a lot of effort. But when flying with Air New Zealand, it feels more convenient and easy. This is because the dedicated services offered by the airline make travelers fly comfortably on any trip. However, for curating a trip with several individuals for leisure or corporate meetings, one can opt for its group travel program. This service allows multiple flyers to travel using a single itinerary. All this is done by making an Air New Zealand group booking request through its simple method. Also, it offers a range of services that make the air travel experience more comfortable. Other than that, a detailed understanding of this is provided in the sections below.

What Is Group Booking on Air New Zealand?

On Air New Zealand, the group booking feature is only offered to flyers willing to travel together. These bookings are easy to access but only require the travelers to align with certain conditions. Further, the tickets for Air New Zealand group travel are provided at a much lower cost and come with a wide range of amenities that the flyers need to share. It helps to arrange all kinds of travel, such as leisure, business meetings, sports events, and more. But passengers belonging to a group of 10 or more members can only avail these special fares. Thus, by adding this feature to air travel, one can enjoy their flights with memorable experiences.

What Are the Benefits of Group Travel on Air New Zealand?

Traveling as a group on Air New Zealand allows flyers to seek maximum perks and benefits. This airline is enriched with exclusive services, including Air New Zealand group discounts, that make the journey enjoyable and inexpensive at the same time. Moreover, the quality of perks provided here leverages the excitement from the start of departure to the end of arrival. Further, one can glance below to know more about their advantages.

  • Enough facilities for everyone under a uniform fare.
  • Name changes are applicable 5 days before the journey date.
  • Personalized service for every member of a group.

How Do I Book a Group Flight on Air New Zealand?

Flyers can have several ways to reserve a group flight on Air New Zealand. They can curate their journey quickly without much effort or any additional cost. These methods include the Air New Zealand Group Travel Portal and the email for shared reservations, which is groupsales@airnz.co.nz. Using any of these methods will provide the tickets with several benefits. To learn the complete procedure for booking from the website, flyers can follow the pointers below.

  1. Navigate to the official website of Air New Zealand.
  2. Hover over the “Book” icon and then select the “Group Travel” option.
  3. Now, click on “Book Group Flights” and move to the new page.
  4. Fill in the form with all the required information.
  5. After a while, the airline will share a quotation.
  6. Accept it before the given timeline and complete the final payment.
  7. Finally, share the group members’ details and get the ticket itinerary sent to the email.

Conclusion

Overall, the success of any group itinerary depends entirely on effective coordination and partnering with the right airline services like Air New Zealand. With its group flights, flyers can plan their trip smoothly with its several booking methods. Moreover, the service and amenities offered make the journey even better. These flights require some conditions to be fulfilled that help to avoid the unwanted hassles. Also, the details of the reservation are illustrated there.
