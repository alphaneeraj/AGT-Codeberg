Air Transat Group Booking

Air Transat Group Booking | Rates | 10+ Guest Quote

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 14, 2026 · 3 min read

Embarking on an escape with Air Transat will lead to an enthralling travel experience. This is due to their range of services that allow travelers to plan a journey for different purposes. One of their offerings is group booking that addresses the concerns of travelers willing to go on a vacation, business trip, and other types of excursions with their companions. By making use of Air Transat group booking, larger parties can also arrange their trips within a planned budget through the available rates. Moreover, flyers can get several benefits, but there are certain factors that must be recognized. The headings highlighted below explain the essentials of this travel option more deeply.

What Are the Air Transat Group Booking Policies?

Passengers flying together with known faces on Air Transat flights get additional travel features. But to avail them, they have to arrange their shared journeys by considering relevant policies. Furthermore, they can learn those rules from the Air Transat Group Travel Page and through the quotation offered after sending a booking request. Also, one can find some essential group booking policies underneath.

  • A group trip can be arranged for a threshold of more than 9 adults traveling on the same flight segment.
  • The airline may allow flyers to request seats several months before departure. But extra charges may apply depending on the available options.
  • Flight changes for reservations may be possible when the booking term allows.
  • The airline may cancel a booking when the party fails to meet the required policies.
  • Passenger details by a group organizer have to be sent within the set deadline.
  • The party members flying together must carry the required valid documents.

How to Do Group Booking on Air Transat?

A group of companions can plan their expedition with ease by requesting a quotation from Air Transat. The way to request it is by calling the airline at 1-866-322-6649 or submitting the online form. Besides, the Air Transat Group Quote Request Form is available on its official website. Travelers opting for this booking channel can acquire the flight tickets securely. Moreover, the steps to do a group booking online are as follows.

  1. Head to the official website of Air Transat.
  2. Find "Book" and click on that option on the home page.
  3. Choose the "Group travel" icon and then "Request a quote online."
  4. After that, answer the questions through the form and press "Next."
  5. Then, share the flight information and click on "Continue."
  6. Further, review the details and tap on the "Continue" button again.
  7. Now, flyers will receive a price quote by email with a specific deadline.
  8. Approve it accordingly and complete the transaction.
  9. As a next step, party member names have to be shared before the due date.
  10. In the end, the tickets will be shared via email.

What Are the Advantages of Group Booking on Air Transat?

Air Transat lets passengers fly in an effortless manner with their family, friends, or companions whom they know. Not only does this airline offer different flight options, but it also offers various travel perks and facilities. With these offerings, scheduling air trips in a group becomes more compelling and elevated. Look at the points below to learn about these group booking advantages.

  • Eligible parties can enjoy complimentary travel benefits.
  • Each group flyer gets one checked bag weighing up to 23 kg.
  • Pay a reduced deposit at the time of booking.
  • Get the possibility of matching a competitor's price.

Conclusion

Thus, a tour for a group can be crafted with ease by choosing Air Transat. Also, travelers opting for the group travel feature of the airline to fly together also receive bespoke amenities that have been elaborated above. Along with this, the booking process, requirements, and guidelines for arranging shared trips are explained here. Still, if flyers have anything to ask about flying in larger numbers, contact the airline.
