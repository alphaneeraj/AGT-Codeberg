How Can I Book a Group Flight Ticket with Flybondi?

By Airlines Group Travel Team · October 23, 2026 · 3 min read

Traveling with companions feels more enjoyable and enriching on a Flybondi flight. But when a number of passengers want to travel on the same flight without creating separate reservations, they need to use its group travel service. It allows them to fly together for a common event with their known members under a single credential. Besides, the Flybondi group travel feature offers such shared journeys to be arranged within budget. But for that, conditions and other requirements related to creating group trips are mandatory to consider. The following sections explain the main factors to know before planning a shared trip with Flybondi.

Does Flybondi Offer Discounts on Group Bookings?

Definitely! Discounts on group bookings are offered by Flybondi. But overall savings may depend on the headcount traveling together and the fare available for the chosen flight. Often, Flybondi group deals are offered on select international and domestic routes. Also, when travelers proceed to secure flight tickets in advance and plan to fly during the days that are less busy, then they will be able to grab group discounts.

How Can I Book a Group Flight Ticket with Flybondi?

Preparing a group excursion with Flybondi becomes easier when the reservation requirements are understood beforehand. Basically, to fly in a group on a Flybondi flight, 15 or more passengers are required. Furthermore, a block of seats for the required number of travelers can be booked here by submitting a Flybondi group booking designated form. It is available on the airline's official website to receive a quotation. Besides, travelers can look below and follow the detailed steps for group reservations.

  1. Head to Flybondi's official website and access its "Groups" section.
  2. The next page provides the form needed to start the inquiry.
  3. Specify the flight route, travel dates, party type, and other requested trip information.
  4. Forward the inquiry to the airline to assess the party's requirements.
  5. Then, Flybondi will respond with a fare proposal, which needs approval within the stated time limit.
  6. Further, follow the payment schedule outlined in the offer to confirm the reservation.
  7. Now, share the passenger list along with the required information from each traveler's documents.
  8. Afterwards, the airline will email the flight tickets to the party leader.

Why Choose Flybondi for Group Travel?

Flybondi is considered a great and reliable airline to fly with companions. Nowadays, this airline has made traveling together more interesting by offering unmatched travel advantages. Furthermore, families, a number of friends, sports teams, and other parties that fly on the same Flybondi group flight with the required number of travelers may access these perks. Look at the points shared below to collect information about the benefits acquired by choosing Flybondi for a collective journey.

  • Special group fares are offered for passengers traveling together.
  • One booking enquiry to manage flights according to the requirements.
  • Flexible payment terms are available, depending on the quotation offered.
  • Passengers get more time to coordinate passenger details before ticket issuance.
  • Group booking assistance is offered for handling reservation-related queries.

Conclusion

Thus, managing the collective flight tickets feels more seamless with Flybondi. Here, one can easily plan any kind of shared journey, such as leisure, business class, sports, and other events. Moreover, Flybondi group rates help passengers to secure their seats without breaking the bank. But there are certain terms and conditions that need to be followed. On the other hand, all the companions can enjoy air travel, which offers a number of perks. To explore them and arrange collective trips seamlessly, they have to consider the information shared above.
